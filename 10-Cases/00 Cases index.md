# Cases

Map of content for every case card in the vault. A case card is 1 real deal, trade or episode from the commodity business, kept for the mechanism it shows and linked to the concept note that explains it.

A concept note says how something works. A case card says when it happened to someone, and what it paid or cost. Keep that line: the idea lives in `01-Concepts/` or `04-Strategies/`, the episode lives here, and they link to each other.

## Format

Every card has the same shape: 1 sentence that says what happened, then 5 lines.

```markdown
---
tags:
  - "#energy/crude"
date-added: "2026-01-01"
---

# Title of the case

One sentence that says what happened.

- **Who:** the house, the trader, the counterparty
- **When:** the year or the dates
- **The trade:** what was bought, sold, moved, stored or financed
- **Why it paid or failed:** the mechanism, with 1 or 2 numbers
- **The concept behind it:** a link to the concept note

## Sources
```

A card carries the domain tag of its market, so it takes the colour of that domain in the graph. Each card is written in the author's own words and cites its source. No passage is copied from a book.

## Working principles

- One card per case, in this folder. This index holds only `[[wikilinks]]`, never the case itself.
- Add the link here when a card is written, under the market it belongs to. A case that spans markets goes under Trading houses and finance.
- Every card links to at least 1 concept note, and that note links back under Related concepts.

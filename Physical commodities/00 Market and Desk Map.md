# Market and Desk Map

The whole commodity complex in 1 page, read 2 ways. Part 1 is the market tree: 4 market hubs, their sub hubs and the benchmarks that trade under each. Part 2 is the desk view: how a trading house actually splits the book into seats, and every trade a seat runs, including the ones that cross into another hub. Part 3 lists the chains that tie hubs together.

The point of the desk view: a crude trader is never only long or short crude. The book also holds grade differentials, time spreads, cracks, tanker freight and storage. Thinking by seat shows which trades sit together on 1 P&L.

A ✓ marks a sub hub with notes in the vault today.

## Part 1. The market tree

### 1. Energy ([[Energy]])

**1.1 Crude oil ✓**
- Light sweet Atlantic: [[WTI Crude Oil]] (Cushing, Midland, Houston), the Brent complex ([[Dated Brent]], [[Brent Crude]], the North Sea basket including WTI Midland)
- Middle East sour: [[Dubai Crude]], Oman, Murban, Basrah, producer official selling prices
- Russia and Caspian: [[Urals Crude]], ESPO, CPC
- West Africa: Nigerian and Angolan grades
- Latin America: Brazilian presalt, Guyana, Mexican Maya
- Canadian heavy: WCS
- Cross cutting: [[Quality Spread]], [[Sweet vs Sour]], [[Brent-WTI Spread]], [[EFS]], [[Storage Economics]]

**1.2 Refined products ✓**
- Light ends: gasoline ([[RBOB Gasoline Futures]], Eurobob, Singapore 92), naphtha (NWE, Japan), blending components
- Middle distillates: diesel ([[ULSD Heating Oil Futures]], ICE Gasoil), jet and kerosene, Singapore gasoil
- Heavy end: HSFO 3.5%, VLSFO 0.5%, vacuum gasoil, bitumen
- Other: petcoke, base oils
- Cross cutting: [[Crack Spread]], [[Gasoline Crack]], [[Heating Crack]], [[Refinery Utilization]], [[Turnaround Season]]

**1.3 NGLs and LPG**
- Propane (Mont Belvieu, Saudi contract price, Far East index), butane, ethane

**1.4 Petrochemicals** (peripheral for the houses)
- Ethylene, propylene, aromatics, methanol

**1.5 Natural gas ✓**
- US: [[Henry Hub Natural Gas]], regional basis hubs
- Europe: [[TTF Natural Gas]], NBP, German THE, Italian PSV, French PEG
- Cross cutting: storage, pipeline capacity, summer winter spreads

**1.6 LNG ✓**
- Benchmarks: JKM, NWE delivered, US Gulf FOB, [[Henry Hub-TTF Spread]]
- Contracts: oil indexed vs hub indexed, destination flexibility
- Assets: liquefaction tolling, regasification capacity

**1.7 Power**
- Europe: Germany, France, Nordics, Italy, Spain, UK
- US: PJM, ERCOT, CAISO, MISO, NYISO
- Products: baseload, peak, day-ahead, intraday, balancing, capacity, ancillary services
- Renewables: PPAs, guarantees of origin, shape and cannibalisation

**1.8 Coal**
- Thermal: API2 (Europe), API4 (South Africa), Newcastle (Asia), Indonesian grades
- Metallurgical coal sits under ferrous (2.3)

**1.9 Environmental products**
- Compliance carbon: EUA, UK ETS, California, RGGI
- Voluntary carbon credits
- Fuel credits: US RINs, California LCFS
- Renewable certificates

**1.10 Biofuels**
- Ethanol, biodiesel (FAME), HVO and renewable diesel, sustainable aviation fuel
- Feedstocks: used cooking oil, tallow, vegetable oils

**1.11 Nuclear fuel** (niche): uranium, conversion, enrichment

**1.12 Hydrogen and ammonia** (emerging)

## Part 2. The desk view

Each seat lists its core book first, then the trades that cross into another hub.

### Energy seats

**Crude oil, Atlantic basin**
- Core: WTI and Brent outrights, [[Brent-WTI Spread]], North Sea and West African differentials to [[Dated Brent]], [[Time Spread]], [[Calendar Spread]]
- Cross hub: refinery margins and [[Crack Spread]], transatlantic Aframax and Suezmax freight, contango storage on land and at sea ([[Floating Storage]])

**Crude oil, East of Suez**
- Core: [[Dubai Crude]] structure, Brent vs Dubai ([[EFS]]), producer official selling prices, [[Sweet vs Sour]]
- Cross hub: VLCC freight on the Middle East to China route, Asian refinery margins, sanctions and the [[Shadow Fleet]]

**Gasoline and naphtha**
- Core: [[Gasoline Crack]], RBOB vs Eurobob arb, summer vs winter grade spread, blending to spec
- Cross hub: naphtha vs LPG as petrochemical feed, East West naphtha spread, MR freight, ethanol blending

**Distillates**
- Core: diesel and jet cracks, ICE Gasoil vs ULSD transatlantic arb, the jet diesel regrade
- Cross hub: East West arb with LR2 freight, heating season, Russian product flows, HVO vs diesel

**Fuel oil and bunkers**
- Core: HSFO vs VLSFO (the Hi-5), Singapore vs Rotterdam arb, fuel oil crack, blending
- Cross hub: [[Scrubber Spread]] that links to freight, bunker supply margins, fuel oil in power generation

**LPG**
- Core: propane and butane outrights, US export arb (Mont Belvieu vs Far East index), Saudi contract price
- Cross hub: VLGC freight, propane vs naphtha in crackers, winter heating

**Pipeline gas**
- Core: [[TTF Natural Gas]] and NBP outrights, summer winter spread, storage optimisation, hub spreads
- Cross hub: gas vs coal switching in power, pipeline capacity, the LNG arb

**LNG**
- Core: JKM vs TTF arb, JKM vs [[Henry Hub Natural Gas]] netback, cargo diversions, oil indexed vs hub indexed contracts
- Cross hub: LNG carrier freight and boil off, destination flexibility valued as an option

**Power, Europe**
- Core: baseload vs peak, day-ahead vs intraday, cross border spreads
- Cross hub: spark, dark and clean spreads, carbon, renewables shape and cannibalisation, PPAs

**Power, US**
- Core: ISO hub prices, nodal and congestion spreads, capacity value
- Cross hub: gas basis into power, weather, battery arbitrage

**Coal**
- Core: API2 vs Newcastle arb, calorific value spreads, Indonesian supply
- Cross hub: coal vs gas switching, Capesize and Panamax freight, the dark spread

**Carbon and environmental**
- Core: EUA outrights and December spreads, EUA vs UKA, policy event trades
- Cross hub: the carbon leg of clean spark and dark spreads, compliance buyer hedging

**Biofuels**
- Core: ethanol vs gasoline, HVO and SAF vs diesel and jet, RINs and LCFS credits
- Cross hub: feedstock spreads (used cooking oil, tallow, palm), the vegetable oil link, sugar and corn into ethanol

### Metals seats

**Copper, refined metal**
- Core: LME outright, [[Cash to 3 Month Spread]], LME vs COMEX vs SHFE arbs, regional premiums
- Cross hub: [[Warrant]] and warehouse plays, scrap vs cathode ([[Scrap Ratio]]), Chinese import arb

**Copper, concentrates**
- Core: concentrate trading and [[TC RC]], blending, payables and penalties
- Cross hub: smelter margins, cobalt and precious by-products, bulk freight

**Aluminium**
- Core: LME outright, regional premiums, the warehouse queue
- Cross hub: alumina and bauxite, energy cost as the floor, tariffs

**Zinc, lead, nickel and tin**
- Core: LME outrights and spreads, zinc and lead concentrates
- Cross hub: nickel pig iron vs class 1 metal, stainless steel demand, the 2022 nickel squeeze

**Iron ore and steel inputs**
- Core: 62% vs 65% grade spread, pellet and lump premiums, SGX vs Dalian
- Cross hub: Brazil vs Australia arb with Capesize freight, coking coal, the steel margin

**Precious metals**
- Core: gold and silver outrights, [[Lease Rate]], location swaps, EFP, [[Gold Silver Ratio]]
- Cross hub: PGM spreads tied to auto demand, refining and bar conversion

**Battery metals**
- Core: lithium carbonate vs hydroxide, spodumene, cobalt hydroxide payables
- Cross hub: offtake and prepayment deals, the thin futures market as a partial hedge

### Agriculture seats

**Wheat**
- Core: class spreads (Chicago vs KC vs Euronext), [[Old Crop New Crop]], weather trades
- Cross hub: US vs Black Sea vs EU origin arb with Panamax and Supramax freight

**Corn**
- Core: CBOT outright and spreads, US vs Brazil second crop, export basis
- Cross hub: corn vs wheat feed switching, corn into ethanol, Panamax freight

**Soy complex**
- Core: [[Crush Spread]], Brazil vs US origin, Chinese demand
- Cross hub: meal into feed, oil into biodiesel, [[Soybean-Corn Spread]]

**Vegetable oils**
- Core: palm vs soy oil spread, rapeseed and sunflower oil
- Cross hub: vegetable oil vs diesel through biodiesel, tanker freight

**Sugar, raw and white**
- Core: raw vs white, the white premium and the refining margin, Brazil vs India supply
- Cross hub: freight on Handysize and Supramax ships

**Sugar and ethanol**
- Core: the Brazilian mill switch between sugar and ethanol ([[Sugar-Ethanol Spread]])
- Cross hub: ethanol parity with gasoline, Brazilian fuel policy

**Coffee**
- Core: [[Arabica-Robusta Spread]], origin differentials, certified stocks, quality grades
- Cross hub: weather and disease, currency of the producing country

**Cocoa**
- Core: [[Cocoa NY-London Spread]], origin differentials, grind margin, [[ICE Cocoa Stocks]]
- Cross hub: [[West African Port Arrivals]], weather and disease

**Cotton**
- Core: ICE outright, US vs Brazil vs Australia origin, on call sales, certified stocks
- Cross hub: Asian mill demand, competition with polyester and therefore oil

**Fertilisers**
- Core: urea, ammonia, phosphate and potash prices and origin arbs
- Cross hub: gas as the cost of nitrogen, grain prices as the driver of demand

**Livestock**
- Core: cattle and hog futures, feeder vs live spread
- Cross hub: feed cost through corn and soy meal

### Freight seats

**Dry bulk, paper**
- Core: FFA outrights, curve trades ([[FFA Curve]], [[FFA Settlement and Rolls]]), size spreads (Capesize vs Panamax), route spreads (C3 vs C5)

**Dry bulk, physical**
- Core: [[Physical vs Paper Freight]], [[TC In and TC Out]], [[Relet]], [[Freight Basis Risk]]
- Cross hub: freight plus cargo trades with iron ore, coal and grain, the [[Scrubber Spread]]

**Dry bulk, options and positioning**
- Core: [[Freight Options]], open interest and positioning reads, roll timing

**Crude tankers**
- Core: VLCC, Suezmax and Aframax routes
- Cross hub: tanker freight inside crude arbs, [[Floating Storage]] in contango, sanctions and the [[Shadow Fleet]]

**Clean tankers**
- Core: LR2, LR1 and MR routes, the crude vs clean freight spread
- Cross hub: product arbs between regions, refinery closures and new refineries

**Gas carriers**
- Core: LNG carrier and VLGC rates
- Cross hub: the LNG and LPG arbs, which open or close on freight

## Part 3. The chains between hubs

- Crude → refinery → products: [[Crack Spread]], [[Process Margin]]
- Gas → power: the spark spread. Coal → power: the dark spread. Carbon sits on both as the clean spreads
- Gas → ammonia → fertiliser → grain costs
- Iron ore plus coking coal → steel: the steel margin
- Soybeans → meal plus oil: the [[Crush Spread]]. Palm and soy oil → biodiesel
- Sugar ↔ ethanol: the Brazilian mill switch
- Freight joins every hub: any arb between 2 regions is the price gap minus freight ([[Location Arbitrage]])

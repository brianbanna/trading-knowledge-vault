# NZD/USD

## Definition

NZD/USD, the "kiwi", is the price of 1 New Zealand dollar in US dollars. On 2026-07-03 it traded 0.5711. It is the market's cleanest liquid proxy for dairy [[Terms of Trade]] through the [[GlobalDairyTrade]] auctions, and it trades on global risk sentiment and [[RBNZ]] policy. The kiwi is highly correlated to [[AUD/USD]]; the AUD/NZD cross is the pure relative trade between the two commodity dollars.

## Why it matters (commodities and FX)

New Zealand is a small, open, agricultural economy, and dairy is its dominant export, so NZD is a [[Commodity Currencies|commodity currency]] geared to soft commodity [[Terms of Trade]] rather than metals or oil. Strong [[GlobalDairyTrade]] auctions, especially [[Whole Milk Powder]] prices, lift export income and support the kiwi. Because New Zealand and Australia share an export destination in [[China]] and similar risk profiles, NZD/USD and [[AUD/USD]] move together most days, and the AUD/NZD cross isolates what is genuinely idiosyncratic: relative dairy versus metals terms of trade, and the [[RBNZ]] versus [[RBA]] policy gap. NZD is also a higher yielding [[Risk-On Risk-Off|risk on]] currency and a frequent [[Carry Trade]] target, so it rallies in calm, yield seeking tapes and sells off when volatility spikes. For a commodity trader it is a liquid way to express a soft commodities and China demand view with a rates overlay.

## Concrete example

**Concrete:** A [[GlobalDairyTrade]] auction prints [[Whole Milk Powder]] up 5% against a flat expectation, and the tape is [[Risk-On Risk-Off|risk on]]. A trader buys NZD/USD 10 standard lots (1,000,000 NZD) at 0.5695. In FX one pip is 0.0001, and on a standard 100K NZD lot that is roughly 10 USD per pip. Spot rallies to 0.5740 and the trader exits: 45 pips × 10 USD × 10 lots is about 4,500 USD. The fail case: the trader holds the kiwi long as a [[Carry Trade]] into an [[RBNZ]] meeting, the bank turns dovish and cuts while a [[Risk-On Risk-Off|risk off]] wave lifts the dollar, and NZD/USD falls from 0.5720 to 0.5665. The stop triggers for a 55 pip loss, about 5,500 USD, and [[AUD/USD]] falls in sympathy. The commodity view was fine, but rates and risk sentiment overrode it.

**Simplified:** NZD/USD is how many US dollars buy 1 New Zealand dollar. It rises when dairy prices are strong, the [[RBNZ]] is hawkish, and risk appetite is high, and it falls in [[Risk-On Risk-Off|risk off]] and when carry unwinds. It shadows [[AUD/USD]] closely. One pip is 0.0001, and on a 100K NZD lot that is about 10 USD. A 100 pip move is roughly 1,000 USD per lot.

## Fundamentals

### Drivers of NZD strength (NZD/USD higher)
- Rising [[GlobalDairyTrade]] auction prices, especially [[Whole Milk Powder]] (positive [[Terms of Trade]] shock)
- [[RBNZ]] hawkish relative to [[Federal Reserve]] (rate differential favors NZD)
- [[Risk-On Risk-Off|Risk on]] environments and [[Carry Trade]] demand for the higher yield
- Strong [[China PMI]] and Chinese demand for New Zealand exports
- Broad USD weakness ([[DXY]] lower)

### Drivers of NZD weakness (NZD/USD lower)
- Falling dairy prices (negative [[Terms of Trade]] shock)
- [[Federal Reserve]] hawkish relative to [[RBNZ]], or dovish [[RBNZ]] surprises
- [[Risk-On Risk-Off|Risk off]] and carry unwinds, where the higher beta kiwi underperforms
- China growth scare weighing on the whole commodity dollar bloc
- Broad USD strength

### Seasonality
Weak. Some tendency for the kiwi to track the dairy auction cycle, with New Zealand's peak milk production season affecting supply and [[GlobalDairyTrade]] prices, but the [[RBNZ]] path and global risk regime dominate any seasonal signal.

## Key data and reports

- [[RBNZ]] Official Cash Rate decisions and Monetary Policy Statements
- [[GlobalDairyTrade]] auction results (twice monthly, [[Whole Milk Powder]] focus)
- New Zealand CPI (quarterly) and labour market data
- [[China PMI]] and Chinese demand indicators
- Australian data and [[RBA]] decisions given the tight [[AUD/USD]] linkage
- US Non Farm Payrolls and [[Federal Reserve]] decisions
- [[CFTC]] IMM positioning: net speculative NZD positions

## Key spreads and relative value

- **AUD/NZD cross:** the pure Australia versus New Zealand relative trade, isolating dairy versus metals and the [[RBNZ]] versus [[RBA]] gap. See [[AUD/USD]].
- **NZD/USD vs 2 year rate differential (NZ 2Y minus US 2Y):** the rates driver behind carry demand.
- **NZD/JPY cross:** a high beta [[Carry Trade]] and [[Risk-On Risk-Off]] expression.
- **NZD/USD vs [[GlobalDairyTrade]] index:** the soft commodity [[Terms of Trade]] channel.
- **[[DXY]] (Dollar Index):** NZD is not in the basket, but the broad dollar still drives the tone.

## Important relationships

- Very high positive correlation with [[AUD/USD]]
- Positive correlation with [[GlobalDairyTrade]] and [[Whole Milk Powder]] prices
- High beta to global [[Risk-On Risk-Off]] and a frequent [[Carry Trade]] target
- Sensitive to [[China PMI]] and Chinese demand for the commodity dollar bloc
- Inverse correlation with the broad USD
